## 22. Naming convention: `name` and `printed`

Two kinds of course name in the brochure cannot be published verbatim — a typo
("Baking and finance") and a level folded into the name ("Diploma in supply chain management",
offered at certificate level on the very next row). Both are handled by the same field, so no
printed wording is ever discarded:

```js
{
  slug: 'banking-and-finance',
  name: 'Banking and Finance',
  printed: { name: 'Baking and finance', reason: 'typo' },
  levels: [...],
}

{
  slug: 'supply-chain-management',
  name: 'Supply Chain Management',
  printed: { name: 'Diploma in supply chain management', reason: 'level-in-name' },
  levels: [{ level: 'Diploma', ... }, { level: 'Certificate', ... }],
}
```

`name` is what the site displays. `printed` records the brochure exactly as set, and is present
only where the two differ — a course printed correctly has no `printed` field at all.

**`printed` is data, not display.** It is never rendered on a page. Its two jobs are:

1. **Audit.** Every published course name traces back to the printed document in one hop, and any
   correction is reversed by deleting one field.
2. **Search.** `printed.name` is folded into the course search index as an alias, so a student
   holding the brochure who types "baking and finance" or "diploma in supply chain management"
   lands on the right course.

The site does not print "your brochure has a typo" at a visitor. It quietly shows the right name
and still answers to the wrong one.

### When to strip a level from a name

Strip the level prefix **only when the course is offered at more than one level.** Where a course
exists at a single level and the name says so, the name is correct and stays:

| Printed | Published | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diploma in supply chain management | Supply Chain Management | Also offered as a certificate |
| Diploma in community &social work | Community and Social Work | Also offered as a certificate |
| Diploma in guiding and counselling | Guidance and Counselling | Also offered as a certificate |
| Higher diploma in human resource management | *unchanged* | Offered at this level only |
| Higher diploma in secretariat | *unchanged* | Offered at this level only |
| Accounting Technician Diploma | *unchanged* | The qualification's own name |

### Why "Baking" is read as "Banking"

Three reasons, recorded so the decision is not re-argued later:

1. KNEC publishes a Banking and Finance syllabus at both diploma and certificate level. It
   publishes no "Baking and Finance".
2. The row sits in the School of **Business**, between Road Transport and Logistics and
   Accountancy, at KCSE C entry with a KNEC exam body — a business-studies row in every other
   respect.
3. Baking is already in the brochure under a different school: **Pastry and Bakery**, School of
   Hospitality, 3 months, KCPE entry (§18). The institute does not list the same subject twice
   under incompatible entry requirements.

Every left-hand entry in §20 becomes that course's `printed.name` with `reason: 'typo'`. The three
rows above take `reason: 'level-in-name'`.
